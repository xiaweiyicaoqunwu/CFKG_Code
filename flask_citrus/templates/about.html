About CFKG

Our aim is to leverage natural language processing technology to integrate multi-source citrus flavor data, construct a comprehensive knowledge graph, and develop knowledge query, flavor reasoning, and question-answering functions based on this graph to support agricultural breeding and flavor research.

How to Use

  • Click Explore KG on the homepage to enter the graph visualization interface.
  • Enter the name of the entity you want to query on the knowledge query page and select the corresponding entity category to perform the query. In addition, you can specify the maximum neighbor hops for the target node, as well as the maximum number of entities each entry can contain. Setting these two parameters too high may result in slower query speeds.
  • For the queried subgraph, clicking on any node or edge will display the corresponding data attributes on the right.
  • On the flavor reasoning page, enter the name of the citrus fruit or compound, and the system will use an advanced neural network to predict the possible flavors of the citrus fruit.
  • Citrus Flavor BOT is a question-and-answer chatbot that responds based on the knowledge in our knowledge graph. You can ask it anything related to citrus, such as citrus flavors, citrus origins, compound content, and more.
